Finance testing is different.

Financial systems are data-heavy, highly integrated, regulated, and business-critical. Standard testing techniques aren't enough. fintest helps testers understand the domain, design better tests, find better defects, and produce stronger evidence required for banking, superannuation, insurance, wealth, and payments.
